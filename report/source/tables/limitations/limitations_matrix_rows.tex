Data source and selection & One retrospective SEER release; substantial exclusion of records with unknown required fields; original reference-study cohort unavailable & The retained cohort may not represent excluded patients, another registry period or another healthcare setting & Larger external cohorts, analysis of missing-data mechanisms and prospectively defined data collection \\
Measurement and timing & Grouped registry variables, combined stage sources and ambiguous treatment codes; predictor availability at the intended prediction time is not always known & Measurement error and use of information recorded after the prediction time remain possible & Time-stamped clinical data and analyses restricted to predictors known when prediction is made \\
Task and outcomes & Diagnosis concerns liver metastasis recorded at presentation; prognosis concerns all-cause survival only among patients already liver-positive & Results do not address later metastasis, cancer-specific survival or all patients with pancreatic neuroendocrine tumours & Prospective cohorts with task-specific, clinically adjudicated outcomes \\
External validation & Only the reproduced reference models were tested externally; UK Erlangen included 30 diagnostic and 11 prognostic patients & Estimates are imprecise and cannot establish subgroup safety, stable thresholds or broad transportability & Larger, temporally distinct and multicentre external validation for every model family \\
Statistical uncertainty & Most intervals resample evaluation patients while holding fitted models fixed and are not adjusted for multiple comparisons & Model-development variability is omitted; individual intervals do not prove clinical superiority or equivalence & Repetition of the complete development process and prespecified rules for multiplicity and equivalence \\
Learned representations & Supervised embeddings were created from the complete cohorts; the prognostic time target did not account directly for censoring & Embedding results show retained information but may overstate performance in previously unseen patients & Representations learned only from training patients with methods designed for censored survival data \\
Synthetic data & Grade 4 was not generated; some linked fields, outcomes and survival times shifted; formal privacy risk was not measured & Generated records are not independent clinical evidence, do not restore rare-group support and are not certified anonymous & Constraint-aware generation plus external utility, fidelity and privacy evaluation \\
Model scope & One MLP design and specific foundation-model versions and procedures were tested & Results do not cover every neural architecture, checkpoint, training dataset or survival-specific model & Broader prespecified model development and external comparison using the same evaluation design \\
Subgroups and clinical use & Brain metastasis and grade 4 are sparse; no clinical threshold or prospective impact study was completed & Rare-group performance, fairness, patient benefit and deployment readiness remain unknown & Subgroup-enriched cohorts, predefined clinical thresholds and prospective impact evaluation \\
